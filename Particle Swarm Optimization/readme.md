Reference Paper: Towards a Modelling, Optimization and Predictive Control Framework for Smart Irrigation
Problem Statement: Smart Irrigation Scheduling Optimization
Consider an agricultural field with:
- 1 agricultural field.
- 5 input parameters (Soil Moisture, Temperature, Humidity, Rainfall, Crop Water Requirement).
- 7 working days.
- 3 available irrigation shifts (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
- Each irrigation shift can supply a variable quantity of water.
- Each crop has a specific daily water requirement.
- Soil moisture levels vary depending on environmental conditions.
- Rainfall contributes to the natural water supply.
- Temperature and humidity influence water evaporation.
- The system must maintain adequate soil moisture for healthy crop growth.
- The algorithm must determine the optimal irrigation quantity and timing while minimizing total water consumption and satisfying crop irrigation requirements.
Objective: Minimize water consumption while satisfying crop irrigation requirements.
